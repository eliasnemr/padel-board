## Description
Explaining a game plan with your hands only goes so far. Padel Board puts the court in your pocket so you can show it instead.

• Doubles and singles courts, drawn to scale
• Drag all four players and the ball
• Break a point into three steps: serve, approach, own the net
• Press play to watch everyone move into position, with arrows showing who goes where
• Your plan is saved on your phone

No ads. No account. Just the court.

## Notes for App Review
Padel Board is a single screen with no login. Drag the numbered players or the ball, tap steps 1–3 to see each stage of a point, or tap the play button to animate all three.
