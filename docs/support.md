# Padel Board support

**Moving players:** drag any numbered player or the ball. Tap the reset button (top right) to put the current step back to its starting positions.

**Steps:** tap 1, 2 or 3 to see each stage of the point. The arrows show where each player moved from the step before. Tap play to watch all three in a row.

**Singles or doubles:** use the switch at the top. Each court keeps its own play.
