# Padel Board privacy policy

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Padel Board doesn't collect any data.

The positions you set on the court are saved only on your iPhone. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

Deleting the app deletes everything it saved.

If a future version handles any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
